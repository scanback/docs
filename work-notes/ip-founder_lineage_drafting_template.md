# Legally-Minded Founder Bio & Lineage Drafting Template

**Document Purpose:** This template provides legally sound phrasing and structural guidelines for founders referencing prior ventures, acquired companies, or defunct products. It is designed to maximize credibility and lineage while minimizing risks related to trademark infringement, nominative fair use challenges, and restrictive covenants.

---

## 1. Core Legal Principles & Strategic Guardrails

### A. Clear Separation of Entities & Classes
* **Distinct Trademark Classes:** Ensure the new brand operates strictly within its designated International Class (e.g., **IC 35** – Advertising & Business Management) without infringing on prior registered classes (e.g., **IC 36** – Financial & Insurance Services).
* **Distinct Naming:** Maintain full separation between the founder's personal name, prior registered marks, and the new operating brand name.

### B. Nominative & Descriptive Fair Use
* **Biographical Factual Statements:** Describing past professional achievements, employment, or products built is protected as descriptive/factual fair use.
* **Functional vs. Proprietary Descriptions:** Refer to the past product by its *function* or *category* (e.g., "an automated financial data platform") rather than relying on proprietary marks or registered trademarks.
* **No Implication of Affiliation:** Ensure the copy never implies that the new venture is sponsored, endorsed, owned by, or affiliated with the previous entity or its acquiring buyer.

### C. Search & Metadata Integrity
* **No Deceptive Schema/JSON-LD:** Do not include former brand names, acquired trademarks, or competitor names in schema markup (e.g., `alternateName`). Keep schema strictly aligned with current legal entities and doing-business-as (DBA) registrations.

## 2. Standard Founder Bio Template (Website "About" Page)

Use this narrative template for website "About Us" pages, founder profiles, or team sections.

```markdown
### About the Founder

[Founder Full Name] is the founder and [Title/Role] of [New Company/Brand Name]. With over [X] years of experience in [Current Industry/IC 35 Domain], [Founder First Name] specializes in [Core Competency / Innovation Area].

Prior to launching [New Company/Brand Name], [Founder First Name] built and successfully exited multiple technology initiatives. In 2015, [Founder First Name] developed and sold a [descriptive phrase of product function, e.g., proprietary financial data and analytics web platform], which operated successfully under its acquiring entity until its retirement in 2020. 

Building on this foundational track record of product innovation, [Founder First Name] established [New Company/Brand Name] to deliver [New Value Proposition / Product Capabilities] for [Target Audience/Clients].
```

## 3. Investor / Pitch Deck History Slide Template

Use this concise template for pitch decks, executive summaries, or fundraising one-pagers.

```markdown
### Proven Execution & Track Record

* **Deep Domain Expertise:** [X]+ years developing high-throughput [industry/technology] solutions.
* **Past Exit & M&A:** Founder and lead architect of a [descriptive category, e.g., specialized web valuation platform] (Acquired in 2015; operational through 2020).
* **Current Venture ([New Brand Name]):** Purpose-built under [IC 35] to address [specific market problem], completely independent of prior legacy architectures.
```
